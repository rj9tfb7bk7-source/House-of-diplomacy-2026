What is this place?

A house for ideas.

House of Diplomacy publishes and preserves young voices across politics, diplomacy, culture and creative work. MUNs are one part of the House, not the whole House.

  1. Read essays, opinions and perspectives.
  2. Create poetry, art, photography and stories.
  3. Participate by joining, collaborating or submitting.

The MUN Archive

The MUN ends.
The ideas don’t.

We preserve the strongest speeches, arguments and moments of political thought that would otherwise vanish after a conference.
